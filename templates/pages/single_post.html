Fresh food lasts longer when it is stored properly, washed at the right time, and kept away from items that speed up ripening.

Plan before you buy

Build a weekly basket around meals you actually cook. Start with vegetables, grains, dairy, fruit, and two or three flexible staples.

Store produce correctly

Keep leafy greens dry, separate ethylene-producing fruit, and use airtight containers for cut produce. Small habits protect freshness and save money.

Good groceries are only useful when they survive long enough to become dinner.

Use your pantry for fast meals: oats, nuts, grains, pasta, sauces, and spices can turn fresh produce into something presentable.
